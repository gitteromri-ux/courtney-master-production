The skeleton

5 moves, every time

Move 1 · 0–5s

Place, not pitch

Open on the world, not the sell. "Paris isn't just a city…" / "In France, refinement is rooted in context." A cultural truth that earns the next 50 seconds.

Move 2 · 5–15s

Meet the expert

"I'm Charline. I teach at French Atelier…" — face + name + authority. Warm, direct, one human. This is the trust anchor.

Move 3 · 15–30s

The method

What you'll actually gain, framed as craft: "across 20 sessions… the words and the confidence to host elegant dinners." Concrete, aspirational, specific.

Move 4 · 30–45s

The invitation

"Join us and learn to speak French the Parisian way." The turn from telling to asking — the soft, elegant CTA.

Move 5 · sign-off

The signature close

The locked bookend: "I'm [Name] — welcome to French Atelier." Identity + brand, said with calm certainty. This is the format's fingerprint. Courtney's equivalent: "I'm Courtney. This is Longevity Life Academy."

The rulebook

What makes them convert

Craft rules (observed)

  • Open on the world, close on the face. Bookend structure — earn attention, then attach it to a person.
  • One expert, direct-adjacent eyeline. Off-lens, never dead to camera. Intimacy over broadcast.
  • Cut on the clause. Every shot change lands with a new idea. WIDE↔TIGHT alternation.
  • Warm, filmic grade. Kodak-Portra warmth; background 1.5–2 stops under the face.
  • Every cutaway is beautiful. No filler. If it isn't gorgeous, it doesn't earn the cut.
  • Signature sign-off. "I'm [Name] — welcome to [Brand]." The bookend fingerprint.

Conversion rules (the CTA layer)

  • Concrete offer number, on-screen. "20 sessions" — a specific, screen-printed promise.
  • Name + authority chyron. One documentary-style lower-third builds instant trust.
  • Aspiration filmed as memory. Show the transformed life, not the product UI.
  • Soft, elegant invitation. "Join us." No hype, no pressure — the tone is the sell.
  • 3 clear CTA cards, ~3 places → the upgrade Courtney's cut demands (see next).

1 · The Noise — problem

Trying to fix your health alone means a thousand voices. Every influencer, every headline — all shouting, all contradicting, all quietly selling. No wonder you're overwhelmed.

2 · The Reset — reframe + promise

So take a breath. Put the noise down. I'm going to show you the direct path to longevity — calmly, with no hype and nothing to push. Let me walk you through it.

3 · Not Your Fault — empathy

Let me tell you why it never stuck. Because it wasn't your fault. Nobody gave you the whole picture — just fragments, one gadget at a time, with no order. That's like building furniture with no instructions. You didn't lack willpower. You lacked a clear path — one you could truly follow. And that path can be taught.

4 · The Six Pillars — mechanism

So here's the whole path, laid out plainly, in six pillars — like a real curriculum, not a pile of tips: nutrition, sleep, movement, strategic supplementation, protect your heart, understand your numbers. You master them in order, each building on the last. Not overwhelming when it's sequenced. Eighteen lessons, week by week — your tailored longevity blueprint.

5 · The Proof — Harvard

And these lessons aren't opinions, or the trend of the month. Harvard tracked 120,000 adults. Five of these very habits, by age 40, added 14 extra years. The science already did the hard part. Our whole job is simply to teach you how to do it.

6 · The Offer — the Academy

That's exactly what the Longevity Life Academy is — a guided classroom, not another pile of tips you'll bookmark and forget. Eighteen live sessions, where you can raise your hand. Real coaching that meets you where you are. And your own numbers, turned into a personal protocol — while someone walks the path with you.

7 · The Close — CTA + sign-off

So here's the honest truth: you really don't need more information. You're already buried in it. You need someone to make it accessible, to show you the right path, and to stay with you while you walk it. Class is in session — and there's a seat for you. I'm Courtney. This is Longevity Life Academy.
